# Mindful Spending: a 45-minute lesson

A shorter version of the [90-minute lesson](lesson-90-minutes.md) for a single class period. The timing matches the one in the app (Workshop → For the facilitator). Preparation, ground rules, the numbers to check and the questions are the same as in the longer version; this page lists only what is done differently.

## What is left out

- the part **When the plan does not add up** (deficit and borrowing): it can be set as homework or used in a following lesson;
- worksheet 2: the leak is worked out aloud (the monthly amount × 12);
- worksheet 4 and the questionnaire are handed out at the end to take home, or two minutes are taken from looking back for them.

## Outline

| Time | Part | What happens | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Introduction and choosing a story | the 50 / 30 / 20 rule, the QR code, pick Ema, Tomáš or Lucie | slides 1–7 |
| 5–13 | 1. Split the income | one-tap split, the first plan items, kinds of expenses | worksheet 1, slides 8–9 |
| 13–21 | 2. Find the leak | which small thing added up most and what it costs in a year | slide 10 |
| 21–28 | 3. Set a goal | how much and how long | worksheet 3, slide 11 |
| 28–35 | 4. An unexpected expense | announce it to everyone at once; where to pay it from, what a fund is for | worksheet 3, slide 12 |
| 35–42 | 5. Looking back | Reflection in the app, in pairs, then briefly as a group | slide 14 |
| 42–45 | The end | everyone taps End and delete | slide 15 |

## Remember

- Keep the introduction short: the three parts of the plan, "Future me first", made-up stories and data that stay on the phone. Slides 3–5 can be shown without much comment.
- In task 4, announce the unexpected expense **to everyone at once**: Ema 2,490 Kč, Tomáš 1,800 Kč, Lucie 4,500 Kč. Lucie's does not fit into Need (she is 200 Kč short), which is a good way into talking about an emergency fund.
- Leave the last three minutes for End and delete. On shared devices, always.
