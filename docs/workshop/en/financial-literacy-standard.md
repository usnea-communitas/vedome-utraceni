# The lesson and the Financial Literacy Standard

Which points of the Czech Financial Literacy Standard (*Standard finanční gramotnosti*) the lesson covers. The Standard was published by the Czech Ministry of Finance on 20 July 2017 (a revision agreed by the Working Group for Financial Education); it sets the target level of financial literacy for pupils of primary and secondary schools. Full text, in Czech: [PSFV_2017_Standard-financni-gramotnosti.pdf](https://mf.gov.cz/assets/cs/media/PSFV_2017_Standard-financni-gramotnosti.pdf), read on 11 November 2026.

## How to read the table

- The **code** is the area (A shopping and paying, B household management, C household budget surplus, D household budget deficit), the level (1.1 primary school, years 1–5; 1.2 primary school, years 6–9; 2 secondary school) and the number or letter of the point in the Standard. The Czech text is quoted word for word; the English translation is ours, not an official one.
- According to the Standard, each higher level includes all the areas of the lower levels, so the primary-school points also apply to secondary school.
- **yes** – the lesson practises the point in an activity; **partly** – the lesson touches the point without covering all of it; **optional** – only in the optional part; **no** – missing from this version of the lesson.

## Expected attitudes and behaviour

Part 5 of the Standard also describes the attitudes and behaviour financial education should lead to. The lesson supports these:

| Point | In the Standard (Czech original · our translation) | Where in the lesson |
|---|---|---|
| b) | řeší finanční problémy včas · deals with financial problems in time | task 4, When the plan does not add up, the pointer to free debt advice |
| c) | má přehled o svých výdajích a příjmech · keeps track of their expenses and income | tasks 1 and 2 |
| d) | žije úměrně svým finančním možnostem · lives within their financial means | task 1, When the plan does not add up |
| e) | rozumí výhodám (finančního) plánování · understands the benefits of (financial) planning | the whole lesson |
| f) | vytváří finanční rezervy a řeší včas zabezpečení na dobu, kdy nebude schopen dosahovat příjmů · builds financial reserves and provides in time for when they cannot earn | task 4 (emergency fund); provision for old age is not covered |
| g) | uchovává důležité doklady a chrání své osobní údaje · keeps important documents and protects their personal data | partly: the data stay on the phone and are deleted at the end; documents are not covered |

## What the lesson does not cover

- **A Shopping and paying**: how prices are set, complaints, unfair commercial practices, cashless payments and cards, contracts, currency exchange, inflation.
- **B 1.2 / 2** a household's balance of assets and liabilities, and **B 1.2 / 3** financial products, apart from the optional look at compound interest.
- **C** saving and investing in depth, insurance, provision for old age.
- **D** loan offers and kinds of credit, creditworthiness, interest calculations, debt collection, debt relief.

These areas can follow in later lessons; the Mindful Spending app does not support them yet.
