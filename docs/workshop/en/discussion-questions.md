# Questions to discuss

Questions for each part of the [lesson](lesson-90-minutes.md). Ask them in pairs first, then let two or three pairs say what they found. The talk is about the people in the stories, not about the participants' own money.

## Introduction

- Do you know the feeling at the end of the month of not knowing where the money went?
- What are the things you cannot do without? And what are the things that make life more pleasant?

## 1. Split the income

- What belongs in Need and what already in Fun? Is a phone a need? And the most expensive plan?
- Why pay Future me at the start of the month rather than from what is left?
- Ema lives with her parents and has spent little of Need so far. Should her ratios differ from Tomáš's? How?
- Which of the person's expenses is fixed, which controllable and which one-off? Why does it matter?
- Is Ema's café job a regular income? What if it ended in the summer? Which income is regular, which irregular and which one-off?

## 2. Find the leak

- What would the small thing cost in a year?
- What could replace it so that the fun stays?
- Is every small thing a leak? When is it fun someone chooses on purpose?
- Which subscription would the person miss least?

## 3. Set a goal

- What happens when a goal has no amount and no date?
- How many months will the person need? What would change with 500 Kč a month more?
- What could hold them up on the way, and how can it be prevented?

## 4. An unexpected expense

- Which part did you pay it from, and what was then short of money?
- How big an emergency fund would cover a surprise like this?
- Lucie's expense did not fit into Need. What now? Where would it be most sensible to take the money from?

## When the plan does not add up

- How can a deficit be solved other than by borrowing?
- Borrow: yes, no, or what does it depend on? (the four situations on slide 13)
- How do I know I can afford the repayments?
- Why are loan offers compared by the APRC (RPSN) and not just by the monthly repayment?

## 5. Looking back

- What could the person do differently next month?
- What from the story also applies to you?
- What will you take into your own plan? (Answered only to yourself, on worksheet 4.)

## To close

- What surprised you today?
- How will your next purchase that is not a need be different?
