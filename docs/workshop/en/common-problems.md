# Common problems and what to do

**Someone has no phone or no data.** Work in pairs or threes. Have everyone open the link at the very start, while Wi-Fi is available: after the first load the workshop also works offline. The lesson can also run entirely on paper: show slide 7; the stories' details are in the appendix of the [lesson](lesson-90-minutes.md#appendix-the-stories-on-paper), and the sums are done on the worksheets.

**The link opens the app without the workshop.** The workshop is recognised by `?workshop=1` in the address. Have participants scan the QR code or type the whole link and open it in the browser. The workshop keeps its own data, so it neither reads nor changes a participant's own plan in the same browser.

**The app is in another language.** The workshop welcome has language buttons. A link with `lang=en` opens English, `lang=cs` Czech.

**Shared tablets or computers.** Always End and delete at the end, or the next group will see a story already started. Before the next group, check that every device starts on the workshop welcome.

**Someone wants to enter their own numbers.** Not in the group. At home they open vedome-utraceni.vercel.app without the workshop link; their data stay only in their browser, are never sent anywhere, and the app can be locked with a PIN. In the lesson, worksheet 4 is enough.

**50 / 30 / 20 does not fit reality.** It is a starting frame. When, for example, rent takes more than half of the income, the ratios can be changed under More → Category ratios. Ask what the person would change and why.

**Participants feel uncomfortable talking about money.** That is why the lesson uses made-up stories. Nobody says their own numbers, and worksheet 4 is not handed in. Do not judge what anyone spends on: Fun is a full part of the plan.

**Someone mentions their own debts or enforcement proceedings.** Do not deal with it in front of the group. Offer to talk after the lesson and point to free debt advice. In Czechia, for example, Poradna při finanční tísni (financnitisen.cz, free helpline 800 722 722); Člověk v tísni also runs debt advice (clovekvtisni.cz).

**Time runs short.** Tasks 1, 3 and 4 come first. The leak can be worked out aloud, and looking back cut to one question. Never skip End and delete.

**Someone finishes early.** Let them change the category ratios and see what happens to the goal; or work out how much sooner the goal would be reached with 500 Kč a month more; or go through another story.

**Questions about investing.** Show the optional part "Future me and compound interest" (in the [lesson](lesson-90-minutes.md)) from your own app. The return is not guaranteed and the calculator is not a recommendation.

**The workshop stayed on a participant's phone.** No harm done: the data are only in their browser and never mix with their own plan. It is still good to delete it: More → End and delete.
