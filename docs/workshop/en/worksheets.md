# Worksheets

Four worksheets for the [lesson](lesson-90-minutes.md), each on one A4 page. Worksheets 1–3 belong to the story in the app; worksheet 4 is for the participant only and nothing on it is handed in. Amounts are net income, what arrives in the account, in Czech crowns (Kč).

- A **fixed** expense is the same every month and cannot be changed in the short term (rent, phone).
- A **controllable** expense is one you decide on as you go (coffee, food delivery, shopping).
- A **one-off** expense comes irregularly, often unexpectedly (a repair, a new appliance).

What else would belong in this person's Need but is missing from the story?
